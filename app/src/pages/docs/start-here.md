# Start here — the 2-minute guide

No account, no sign-up. Your wallet is your login, and everything below is
free except gas. **The only fee is 2% only when something sells — 1.5%
platform, 0.5% keeper.**

## 1. Connect

Click **Connect wallet** in the header and approve the connection in your
wallet. That's it — you're "logged in".

## 2. Get test FLR

On the Coston2 test network, tokens are free and have no value. Grab some
from the [Coston2 faucet](https://faucet.flare.network/coston2) — they
arrive in seconds and cover everything you'll do here.

## 3. Buy, list, or offer

- **Buy** — open any listing and click **Buy**. You pay exactly the shown
  price and the NFT lands in your wallet in the same transaction.
- **List** — open an NFT you own and click **List for sale** (or **Start
  auction**). Listing is free; set a price and a duration. Cancel any time
  with **Cancel listing** — the NFT never leaves your custody until it sells.
- **Offer** — on collections that allow it, click **Place offer**. Your
  money is held safely on-chain and refunded in full if the offer isn't
  accepted. Change your mind with **Withdraw offer (full refund)**.

Outbid on an auction? **Withdraw my bid** returns your money. Funds are
never lost — anything refundable shows up on your profile's **Refunds**
card.

## The badges

| Badge | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ✓ Listed collection | From a collection MagicWebb tracks. Its details are still being checked. |
| ✓ Verified | Standard NFT contract and metadata confirmed. Not a judgement of the art or the seller. |
| ✓ Authentic | Verified, and the creator's address is known. |
| ★ Creator | Sold by the collection's creator. |

## Fees, one more time

Listing, auctioning, bidding and offering are free (gas only). You pay
**2% only when something sells — 1.5% platform, 0.5% keeper** — and the
seller pays it, not the buyer.
